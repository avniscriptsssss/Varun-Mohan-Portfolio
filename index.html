INTRODUCTION

About Varun Mohan

Varun Mohan is a technology entrepreneur and software engineer known for his work in artificial intelligence, developer tools and large-scale computing systems. He studied Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), where he completed both bachelor's and master's-level engineering studies. Before becoming a founder, Mohan worked in software and infrastructure engineering, including at Nuro, where he worked on infrastructure related to autonomous vehicles. In 2021, he co-founded Exafunction with Douglas Chen. The company initially focused on GPU virtualization and infrastructure for machine-learning workloads. As generative AI developed rapidly, the team shifted its focus toward software development tools. This pivot resulted in Codeium, an AI-powered coding assistant designed to help developers with tasks such as code completion, code generation and conversational assistance. Codeium later evolved into the broader Windsurf platform, including an AI-native development environment. Mohan's work demonstrates how expertise in systems engineering, artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship can be combined to create practical tools for modern software development.
